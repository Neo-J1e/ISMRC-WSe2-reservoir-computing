# Supplementary Figure 15 — caption

**Supplementary Figure 15 |** Time-domain photoresponse of the floating-gate node with different external node capacitances. The gate mask pattern is held at 1111 (solid lines) or 0000 (dashed lines); the corresponding gate levels are $V_\mathrm{GS}$/$V_\mathrm{GD}$ = −3 V/+3 V and +3 V/−3 V, respectively. Colors denote the external node capacitance: 100 nF (blue), 10 nF (red) and 1 nF (orange). The grey band marks the illumination window (5–11.4 s). External discharge resistor 70 MΩ; laser drive current 80 mA; sample interval 40 ms.

| Node capacitance | Gate mask | τ (s) | R² |

| 10 nF | 1111 | 0.738 | 0.9980 |
| 1 nF | 1111 | 0.155 | 0.9945 |
| 100 nF | 0000 | 7.02 | 0.9987 |
| 10 nF | 0000 | 0.727 | 0.9998 |
| 1 nF | 0000 | 0.154 | 0.9960 |

tau is obtained from a single-exponential fit of $|V - V_\mathrm{dark}|$ after light-off, with the asymptote taken from the 1 s before light-on; R^2 is that of the log-linear fit.
